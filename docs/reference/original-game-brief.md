I would build the whole game around the visual language of the image we created—**isometric battlefield, extremely powerful elemental attacks, huge lightning strikes, enemies flying across the arena, dark environments illuminated by magic, cinematic bosses, and a serious character-driven story**.

# **STORMCROWN: THE SHATTERED OATH**

**Genre:** Isometric Action RPG / Character Action / Dark Fantasy
**Tone:** Epic, dark, emotional, violent, mysterious
**Campaign:** \~15– hours main story, \~45–55 hours including character quests and optional bosses
**Playable characters:** 11
**Main regions:** 5
**Party system:** Take 3 characters into battle and instantly switch between them
**Core fantasy:** *You are not casting little spells—you are controlling forces capable of destroying the battlefield.*

> **Tagline:** *“The sky was never our home. It was our prison.”*

---

# The world

The game takes place in **Aetherra**, a civilization built on enormous landmasses floating thousands of feet above an endless storm.

For over a thousand years, the islands have remained suspended because of an ancient object called the **Stormcrown**.

The Stormcrown controls five primal forces:

**Storm. Flame. Frost. Earth. Void.**

Every kingdom believes it is an ancient machine created by their ancestors.

They are wrong.

The Stormcrown is actually a **prison**.

Something is alive inside it.

Seventeen years before the game begins, an event called **The Crownfall** shattered the Stormcrown into five enormous fragments.

Entire cities fell from the sky.

Millions died.

Lightning storms have never stopped since.

Creatures called **Shardborn** began appearing wherever fragments landed.

And slowly, the floating world itself has started to descend.

At the current rate, Aetherra has less than **one year before its kingdoms fall into the storm below.**

---

# The antagonist

The ruler of the remaining empire is **Emperor Malthren Vane**.

Initially, he appears to be the classic tyrant.

His armies invade neighboring regions.

He kidnaps elemental wielders.

He excavates ancient temples.

He massacres resistance groups.

And he is searching for all five pieces of the Stormcrown.

His plan is called:

## **The Ascension**

Malthren intends to reconstruct the Stormcrown and restore the floating world.

But there is a problem.

Reactivating it requires an enormous amount of living energy.

Approximately **two million people must die**.

Malthren knows this.

And he accepts it.

His philosophy is simple:

> Sacrifice two million now, or allow everyone to die later.

The heroes spend half the game trying to stop him.

Then they discover something worse.

**He is telling the truth.**

Without the Crown, the world really will fall.

From that moment onward, the story stops being simply about defeating the villain.

The question becomes:

### How do you save the world without becoming him?

# The protagonist

## **1. Rael Corven — The Stormblade**

Rael begins the game as a young soldier protecting the independent city of Stormreach.

He fights using a sword containing a strange blue crystal.

During the opening attack on the city, Rael is struck directly by an enormous lightning bolt.

Instead of dying, he absorbs it.

That is when everyone discovers the secret his mother died protecting.

Rael doesn't simply possess Storm magic.

### Part of the Stormcrown is living inside him.

His combat style is extremely aggressive.

Fast sword strikes, teleport-like lightning dashes, aerial launches, chain lightning and enormous area attacks.

His ultimate ability is:

### **Judgment Tempest**

Rael raises his sword.

The battlefield darkens.

Enemies hear thunder.

Then dozens of lightning bolts begin striking enemies around him while a gigantic electrical circle spreads beneath his feet.

The final bolt hits Rael himself.

He absorbs it.

Then releases everything outward in one enormous explosion.

It's essentially the type of attack shown in your reference image.

# Character relationships matter

The eleven aren't simply heroes standing around in a hub.

Some genuinely hate each other.

Sera's entire family was killed by imperial soldiers commanded by Lucen.

Vex secretly worked for Malthren before joining the group.

Mira believes using Void magic should be punishable by death, while Maelin survives by using precisely that kind of forbidden power.

Brann views Rael as reckless and unfit to lead.

Nym has seen visions suggesting Rael himself will eventually destroy Aetherra.

Torren doesn't care about saving human kingdoms because humans destroyed his homeland.

And Lucen believes Rael is naive because he hasn't seen the things Lucen has seen.

The party therefore evolves from:

**Strangers → uneasy allies → fractured group → family.**

Character missions aren't filler.

Completing them changes dialogue, combat abilities and certain story events.

# The central gameplay idea

You enter each mission with **three characters**.

For example:

**Rael + Brann + Nym**

You directly control one.

The other two fight alongside you.

At any moment you can instantly switch.

Imagine Rael fighting six enemies.

He launches one into the air.

You switch to Kes.

Kes shoots the airborne enemy.

Switch to Brann.

Brann smashes three approaching soldiers away.

Switch back to Rael.

Rael teleports into the group and calls down lightning.

The goal is for fights to feel almost choreographed when the player becomes good.

---

# Combat philosophy

There are five fundamental actions:

**Attack → Heavy Attack → Ability → Dodge → Parry**

But characters interact with those systems very differently.

Rael rewards aggression.

Brann rewards perfect parries.

Vex rewards attacking enemies from behind.

Nym rewards battlefield control.

Sera becomes stronger as she remains at low health.

Mira protects teammates and converts blocked damage into radiant energy.

Torren builds rage and transforms.

Kes specializes in ranged weak points.

Maelin becomes stronger when enemies die.

Oren deploys traps and magical constructs.

Lucen is difficult to master but combines mobility, counters and enormous electrical damage.

---

# Elemental reactions

The really spectacular combat comes from combining characters.

Imagine Nym freezes six enemies.

You switch to Brann.

He slams his hammer down.

### SHATTER

All six frozen enemies explode into ice fragments.

Sera covers enemies in fire.

Kes creates a tornado.

### FIRESTORM

The tornado ignites and travels around the arena.

Maelin covers enemies with Void corruption.

Mira attacks them with Light.

### ECLIPSE

A massive Light/Void implosion occurs.

And Rael's lightning hitting enemies standing in water creates:

### CHAIN SURGE

Electricity jumps through every wet enemy nearby.

This makes party composition matter enormously.

The opening region.

A fortress city constructed around enormous lightning towers above a permanent storm.

Heavy rain.

Lightning towers.

Ash storms.

# 3. The White Cathedral

A kingdom completely frozen during Crownfall.

People.

Soldiers.

Children.

Animals.

All frozen exactly where they were seventeen years ago.

# 4. The Verdant Maw

A gigantic forest growing across the skeleton of an ancient floating city.

A gigantic corrupted organism buried beneath the entire region.

# The storyline structure

1. **Act I — The Sky Breaks.** Stormreach is invaded. Rael discovers his powers, meets Sera, Brann and Nym, and learns Malthren is searching for Crown fragments. Lucen appears wearing an Imperial mask and nearly kills Rael. Rael recognizes his brother's fighting style but refuses to believe it.
2. **Act II — Five Broken Kingdoms.** The party travels across Aetherra searching for Crown fragments before Malthren can obtain them. The roster gradually expands. Each region reveals that Crownfall wasn't an accident and that every kingdom helped create the catastrophe through political greed.
3. **Act III — The Truth of the Crown.** The heroes discover Malthren's plan. Worse, they discover his calculations are correct: the floating world has months remaining. Simply stopping him condemns everyone. Vex is exposed as an Imperial spy, the group fractures, and Lucen finally reveals his identity to Rael.
4. **Act IV — Brothers of the Storm.** Malthren acquires four fragments. Lucen begins questioning him after discovering Malthren plans to sacrifice entire civilian populations. Rael and Lucen fight in a massive one-on-one duel during an electrical storm. Rael eventually refuses to kill him. Lucen turns against the Empire and becomes playable.
5. **Act V — The Last Oath.** All eleven heroes invade the Crown Above while five kingdoms fight simultaneously below. Malthren reconstructs the Stormcrown and begins the Ascension. The heroes defeat him—but activating the Crown awakens the entity imprisoned inside it. The final enemy isn't Malthren. It's the ancient being humanity imprisoned thousands of years ago: **Astra Veyr, the Living Storm.**

# Boss philosophy

Bosses should rarely just be enlarged enemies.

A boss battle changes the battlefield.

The Thunder Colossus destroys arena sections.

The Ash King causes lava to rise.

Saint Avel freezes time.

The World Beast runs across multiple interconnected arenas.

Lucen creates moving lightning walls.

Malthren changes the elemental rules of the battlefield.

Astra Veyr destroys entire floating platforms.

So the player isn't simply watching a health bar decrease.

They're surviving an event.

---

# Enemy variety

Regular combat would mix enemy archetypes rather than constantly throwing identical soldiers at you.

**Imperial Legionaries** use shields and formations.

**Tempest Knights** teleport using electricity.

**Shardborn** mutate based on nearby elements.

**Hollow Priests** resurrect dead enemies.

**Ash Brutes** become stronger while burning.

**Void Stalkers** temporarily disappear.

**Sky Reavers** attack from flying creatures.

**Crown Guardians** use the same elemental abilities as playable characters.

By late game you might have fifteen to twenty enemies simultaneously attacking while elites force you to prioritize targets.

---

# Progression

I wouldn't turn this into a game where you replace your sword every five minutes because another sword has `+3% damage`.

Every character instead owns a **signature weapon**.

Rael always has his Stormblade.

Sera always has her chains.

Brann always has his hammer.

You upgrade and transform those weapons through story events.

For example Rael's sword progresses visually from:

**Broken military sword → Stormblade → Tempest Fang → Crownshard → Oathbreaker**

Each transformation changes abilities as well as appearance.

Characters also have three specialization paths.

Rael could evolve toward:

**Storm Duelist** for speed.

**Thunder Lord** for huge spell damage.

**Tempest Guardian** for counters and defensive lightning.

Players can combine abilities across those paths rather than being completely locked into one.

# Character combination attacks

Relationships unlock special attacks.

Rael + Sera:

### **Thunderfire**

Rael sends electricity through Sera's chain blades and she spins them around the battlefield, producing rings of electrical fire.

Rael + Lucen:

### **Twin Tempest**

The brothers jump to opposite ends of the battlefield.

Both throw their weapons into the sky.

A giant lightning storm connects them.

Then they charge toward each other and collide in the center, creating an enormous electrical explosion.

Nym + Brann:

### **Frozen Earth**

Brann raises gigantic stone spikes.

Nym instantly freezes them.

Brann shatters them.

Thousands of frozen fragments tear through surrounding enemies.

These moves also provide an emotional gameplay payoff because many only unlock after characters repair their relationships.

Between missions, the party travels aboard **The Warden**, an ancient flying warship.

This is where characters live.

You can speak with them.

Train.

Upgrade weapons.

Choose missions.

Start character quests.

Nym teaching Torren to read ancient writing.

Vex playing cards with Kes.

# Endgame

After completing the story, players unlock **Crownfall Mode**.

The five regions change.

Storms become more violent.

High-level Shardborn invade earlier areas.

Secret bosses appear.

There are also large combat arenas called **Storm Trials** where players build a three-character team and fight increasingly ridiculous enemy combinations.

The final optional challenge would be:

### **The Eleven**

You choose three heroes.

The other **eight playable heroes become bosses**.

You fight them one after another, eventually facing Rael and Lucen together.

Winning unlocks the strongest cosmetic armor and an alternate ultimate animation for every character.

The thing that would make **STORMCROWN** stand out isn't merely having eleven characters or flashy lightning. It would be the combination of **spectacle + extremely responsive combat + a cast that actually changes throughout the story + a villain whose solution is horrifying but logically valid**.

And visually, the rule for the entire game would be:

**When a powerful ability activates, the whole world should react.**

Lightning illuminates the arena. Fire reflects from armor. Ice changes the floor. Thunder shakes debris loose. Wind moves banners and fog. Enemies get launched, dragged, shattered, burned and thrown into scenery.

That's how I'd turn the image we created into a **real full-scale game rather than simply a pretty combat demo**.
